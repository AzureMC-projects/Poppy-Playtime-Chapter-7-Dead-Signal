# Dead Signal — Game Design

## Vision

A tense first-person horror adventure set inside an abandoned Playtime Co. signal-processing sector. The game should feel industrial, lonely, readable, and increasingly unsafe.

## Core loop

1. Explore.
2. Observe the environment.
3. Find a way to restore or reroute power.
4. Use the GrabPack and machinery to solve a physical puzzle.
5. Discover story evidence.
6. Trigger a new threat or environmental change.
7. Decide whether to hide, run, or keep investigating.

## Act 1 target

**Act 1 — The Signal**

- Opening arrival at Sector 07.
- Establish the abandoned facility and the transmission.
- Introduce the player's basic movement and flashlight.
- Teach the first environmental interaction.
- Introduce the GrabPack prototype.
- Complete the first power-routing puzzle.
- Reveal evidence of Project: Echo.
- Give the player their first unmistakable encounter with Morrow.
- End on a strong transition into the deeper facility.

Act 1 is considered complete only when it plays as a connected sequence rather than a collection of test rooms.

## Horror principles

- Prefer anticipation over constant jumpscares.
- Let the player notice small changes before explaining them.
- Use sound and lighting to direct attention.
- Avoid making the monster feel omnipresent too early.
- Give puzzles physical consequences in the environment.

## Asset direction

All original project assets should have a consistent industrial visual language: worn factory materials, faded safety markings, practical machinery, directional lighting, and restrained colour accents.

Official Poppy Playtime characters, logos, models, textures, music, and other copyrighted material should not be copied into the repository unless their use is explicitly authorized.
